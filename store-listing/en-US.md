## Google Play

Short description (80 characters maximum):

Track part-time shifts, deliveries, payments, expenses and net earnings.

Full description:

Part Time Earnings is a private work and income tracker for people working shifts across stores, delivery services and other part-time jobs.

Record each shift with its date, employer, start and end time, hourly rate and delivery count. The app calculates hourly earnings and delivery bonuses automatically, then organizes the results by date and store.

Keep a clear record of expenses and payments received, review monthly summaries, compare earnings between employers and understand your total and net income. Your information is linked to your account so you can access it from supported devices.

Features:
- Track store and delivery shifts
- Automatic hours and earnings calculations
- Configurable hourly and per-delivery rates
- Earnings grouped by employer or store
- Expense and payment tracking
- Monthly summaries and recent activity
- Private user accounts and password reset
- Automatic saved versions for accidental-change recovery
- Profile management and permanent account deletion

Part Time Earnings does not contain advertising and does not sell personal information.

Review notes:
