Part Time Earnings Privacy Policy

Effective: 10 November 2026

Information we store

We store account information you provide (name, email address, phone number, city and address), authentication records, and your work records including shifts, deliveries, earnings, expenses, payments, notes and settings.

How information is used

Your information is used to provide account access, synchronize your records across devices, calculate summaries, maintain recoverable data versions, administer accounts, and send requested password-reset emails. We do not sell your personal information.

Storage and service providers

Application hosting is provided by Cloudflare and the PostgreSQL database is provided by Neon. Requested password-reset email delivery is provided through Resend. Information is transmitted over HTTPS and account passwords are stored as one-way hashes.

Retention and deletion

Shifts, expenses and payments are automatically removed once their record date is more than 50 days old. Recoverable saved versions are also cleaned up daily and deleted after 50 days. If an account has no activity for 50 days, its account and current tracker records are automatically deleted. Because cleanup runs daily at 03:00 UTC, deletion may occur within 24 hours after the 50-day point. You can also permanently delete your account and its associated tracker data from Profile & Settings → Delete my account inside the app, or request deletion by emailing admin@gmail.com.

Your choices

You can review and edit your profile information in the app. You may request access, correction, or deletion by contacting admin@gmail.com.
