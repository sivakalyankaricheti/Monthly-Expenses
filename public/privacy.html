Part Time Earnings Privacy Policy

Effective: 25 August 2026

Information we store

We store account information you provide (name, email address, phone number, city and address), authentication records, and your work records including shifts, deliveries, earnings, expenses, payments, notes and settings.

How information is used

Your information is used only to provide account access, synchronize your records across devices, calculate summaries, maintain recoverable data versions, administer accounts, and send requested password-reset emails. We do not sell your personal information.

Storage and service providers

Application and database hosting are provided through Render. Requested password-reset email delivery is provided through Resend. Information is transmitted over HTTPS and account passwords are stored as one-way hashes.

Retention and deletion

We retain your information while your account is active. You can permanently delete a normal user account and its associated tracker data from Profile & Settings → Delete my account inside the app. You may also request deletion by emailing admin@gmail.com.

Your choices

You can review and edit your profile information in the app. You may request access, correction, or deletion by contacting admin@gmail.com.
